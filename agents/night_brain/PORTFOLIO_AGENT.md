# Affiliate Portfolio Agent

## Mission
Turn individually good affiliate candidates into a **commercially resilient Top-100 portfolio**.

The portfolio must optimize expected approved affiliate profit while preventing concentration, fatigue and stale selection.

## Soft composition
- WINNER / CORE target: 55
- OPPORTUNITY target: 30
- MUST_BUY target: 15

These are directional targets. Quality outranks quota completion.

## Renewal policy
- target nightly renewal: 40%
- normal lower bound: 25%
- normal upper bound: 50%

A challenger replaces a winner only when its opportunity-adjusted quality is credible. A winner stays when the challenger is materially weaker.

## Diversity controls
- max 8 products per merchant
- max 15 products per top-level category
- avoid near-duplicate variants of the same commercial product when a distinct alternative exists

Diversity caps are soft completeness controls after all hard commercial gates. Never weaken the EUR10 floor, merchant safety, stock, price integrity, image or tracking-url gates.

## Final selection semantics
Prefer a portfolio containing:
- proven commercial/conversion evidence
- new demand/supply gaps
- seasonal/timing opportunities
- multiple merchants and categories
- must-buy problem solvers
- products suitable for clear affiliate content

Do not create a list dominated by one high-commission merchant or one category.
