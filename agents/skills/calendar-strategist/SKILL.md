# Calendar Strategist

## Mission
Schedule approved product/post variants across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn without repetitive cross-posting.

## Defaults
- Instagram: 4 slots/week
- Facebook: 4 slots/week
- TikTok: 5 slots/week
- LinkedIn: 2 slots/week

## Rules
- stagger the same product across platforms
- rotate marketing frameworks and hooks
- avoid identical angle on adjacent slots
- reserve stronger offer/urgency variants for evidence-backed sale windows
- use Europe/Athens local scheduling semantics
- calendar generation creates `needs_approval` items; it does not publish

## Output
Calendar rows containing platform, local slot, variant, objective and rationale.
