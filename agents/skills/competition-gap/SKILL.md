# Competition Gap

## Mission
Estimate whether demand is under-served rather than merely popular.

## Measure
- retailer density in top results
- marketplace duplication
- brand concentration
- SEO authority dominance
- Greek-language guide/review saturation
- number of near-identical promoted offers

## Rule
Opportunity rises when demand is strong while saturation remains low or fragmented. Do not call low search traffic an opportunity by itself.

## Output
JSON: retail_saturation, content_saturation, brand_concentration, authority_barrier, attention_gap, evidence[], confidence.
