# Gap Validator

A pain is not automatically a gap. Compare the pain against direct competitors, substitutes, availability, pricing, usability and existing solution coverage. The model may summarize evidence but never assigns the final numeric score.

Database gates: validated gap >=70 with at least 3 competitors audited; strong gap >=85 with at least 3 competitors and at least 8 evidence items; exceptional >=95 with at least 5 competitors and at least 15 evidence items. Contradictions apply a penalty.

Return solution requirements that are measurable and tied to evidence, not generic feature wishlists.
