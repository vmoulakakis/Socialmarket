# Product Gap Fit

Start with availability, validity, tracking, merchant trust and other hard product gates. Retrieve candidates with lexical/vector search and rerank locally. Score requirement coverage, semantic fit, evidence fit, price, availability, merchant trust and differentiation deterministically.

A high semantic similarity alone is not proof that a product solves the gap. Recommendations require a validated gap plus verified product attributes. Until vector/feature verification passes, keep the match as `candidate` or `needs_review`.
