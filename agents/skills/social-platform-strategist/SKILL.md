# Social Platform Strategist

## Platform logic
- **Instagram:** visual-first 4:5 feed creative; concise hook, scannable body, save/share-worthy utility. QR is optional when legible.
- **Facebook:** problem/offer clarity, direct CTA, useful context. Prefer fewer hashtags.
- **TikTok:** 9:16, fast hook, short caption/script direction. Do not bake affiliate QR/URL into the visual by default.
- **LinkedIn:** professional/useful framing, decision rationale or business/productivity use case; avoid consumer-ad tone unless context justifies it.

## Cross-platform rule
Never publish the exact same copy everywhere. Preserve the core promise while changing hook, length, CTA and creative direction per platform.

## Output
Platform constraints plus recommended `media_format`, `hook_style`, `caption_style`, `cta_style`, `qr_allowed` and `cadence_weight`.
