# SocialMarket Commercial Portfolio Types

Status: **Canonical v1**  
Effective: **2026-09-06**

The three production selectors are intentionally different portfolios. They must not all be referred to generically as “the Top 100”.

| Portfolio key | Display name | Commission gate | Additional gates | Canonical workflow |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| `core_affiliate_top100` | Core Affiliate Top 100 | `>= €10` hard minimum; bounded gate may raise it | Night Brain commercial/evidence policy | `.github/workflows/product-intelligence-v1.yml` |
| `greek_scarcity_top100` | Greek Scarcity Top 100 | `> €20` | Greece scarcity `>= 0.60`, max 5 categories | `.github/workflows/top100-daily-v2.yml` |
| `semantic_marketplace_200` | Semantic Marketplace 200 | `> €30` | max 200 total, max 3 per merchant/seller, quality/affinity floors | `.github/workflows/semantic-marketplace-200.yml` |

The machine-readable repository contract is `config/commercial_portfolios.json` and the production database contract is `intel.commercial_portfolio_types`.

## Ownership

All three portfolios are SocialMarket-owned commercial selectors. SocialScheduler may execute approved publishing intents produced from them, but must not reinterpret or merge their economic gates.

## Persistence rule

Any ranking, selection, handoff or approved publishing intent generated by one of these selectors should carry `portfolio_key` whenever its schema supports metadata. Historical rows without a key must be interpreted from their run/workflow identity; do not guess solely from commission value.

## Naming rule

Dashboards, reports and artifacts should use the explicit display name or `portfolio_key`. The phrase “Top 100” by itself is insufficient because it can refer to two different 100-item portfolios with different eligibility rules.
