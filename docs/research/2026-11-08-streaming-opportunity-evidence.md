# Greek opportunity evidence for streaming discovery — 2026-11-08

Status: CREATED; public research only. Target: Greek-speaking buyers in Greece. Scope: five problem/solution hypotheses, with international category checks and explicit attempts to disprove Greek scarcity. No catalogue ingestion, account access, purchase, publishing, or production verification was performed in this research task.

## Decision

**No hypothesis below is a verified eligible product or a demonstrated high-demand/low-competition winner.** The strongest next research question is narrowly defined tank-filled dishwashers for renters without a water connection, followed by a complete retrofit water-leak shutoff use case. Both have existing Greek alternatives. Window-cleaning robots, automatic cat toilets, and portable power stations provide useful positive demand signals but strong contrary evidence against broad Greek supply scarcity.

Treat these as discovery seeds after the user's sole initial commercial filter, **calculated commission >= EUR15**. Do not impose these five themes as an ingestion allowlist. Do not infer eligibility from retail price. No current affiliate rate, qualifying commission basis, product-level payout, GR delivery quote, or validated tracking URL was available in this research; eligibility therefore remains **unavailable** for every example.

Repository context inspected read-only: `docs/COMMERCIAL_PORTFOLIOS.md`, `docs/TOP100_USER_POLICY_LOCK.md`, `config/top100-selection-v2.json`, `config/semantic-marketplace-200.json`, and `config/demand-intelligence-v3.json`. Older EUR10/EUR20/EUR30 portfolio policies are historical context, not evidence that this newly requested stream should use those thresholds. Existing configuration correctly warns that absence on one marketplace is not scarcity, and that unknown evidence must not become zero.

## Method and limits

Research date for all observations: **2026-11-08 UTC**. Sources were retrieved through public web search and public page opening. Search results and opened pages can reflect different crawl dates; they are not guaranteed live snapshots. Queries included Greek problem phrases, model names, marketplace filters, and English category terms. Broad marketplace pages were cross-checked with specialist or major retailers where practical. Search-result composition is qualitative evidence, not a localized Google rank audit, search-volume estimate, traffic estimate, sales count, or keyword-difficulty score.

Evidence labels: **observed** means visible source content; **inferred** means analyst interpretation; **calculated** means arithmetic from stated inputs; **unavailable** means not established. Reviews indicate historical engagement, not current sales velocity. Marketplace listing/store counts can include variants, duplicates, misclassification, imports, or unavailable offers. Supplier marketing identifies a claimed use case, not independently measured consumer demand. Community anecdotes are self-selected and sometimes old.

## 1. Tank-filled dishwasher for a rental kitchen

**Observed:** HAVA's FAQ describes pitcher filling, draining into a sink or container, European voltage, plate-size limits and maintenance. Its indexed Amazon UK product page names a 5L tank and no-hookup positioning; direct opening failed with HTTP 503, so listing price, review totals and GR delivery were not verified. Greek Reddit search evidence describes dishes accumulating for a one-person household and small households, although that conversation dates to 2024. This triangulates a real job with a solution format; it does not establish large Greek demand.

**Contrary evidence:** Kotsovolos' DeLonghi DDW2PO19 product page already describes independent tank operation and displays 14 reviews. An Insomnia used listing dated 2026-08-30 describes owning the same format since 2020. This falsifies a claim that the product type is new or absent in Greece. Current new-stock availability was not established. Generic countertop dishwashers are not necessarily tank-filled substitutes; inspect manuals before matching them.

**Search terms:** `πλυντήριο πιάτων χωρίς παροχή νερού`, `πλυντήριο πιάτων με δεξαμενή`, `πλυντήριο πιάτων χωρίς σύνδεση βρύσης`, `DDW2PO19`, `DDW2PS19`, `HAVA R01`, `HAVA R09`, `countertop dishwasher built-in water tank`, `no hookup dishwasher`.

**Content competition inference:** broad Greek queries returned established appliance categories, while exact feature/model queries exposed a retailer page, manuals, classifieds and discussions. A feature-specific explanation may be useful; lower SEO competition is unproven. **Next test:** enumerate currently purchasable tank-filled equivalents across five Greek surfaces, inspect 10 distinct buyer questions/reviews, and compare capacity, counter clearance, drainage, hard-water handling, warranty and delivered price. Falsify the gap if several in-stock local equivalents already meet the same constraints at equal or lower landed cost.

## 2. Retrofit water shutoff for an unattended property

**Observed:** Aqara's EU product page describes a controller fitted onto existing valves, requiring a separately sold Aqara Zigbee hub and compatible sensor automation. Greek specialist Kooqie lists the same model and fitting requirements. iStorm displays EUR69.90, but its extracted page contains both available and out-of-stock text; stock is ambiguous. BestPrice and Skroutz also surface the exact product.

**Inference:** the useful offer is a compatibility-led system explanation for water leaks while away. Consumer pain magnitude was not independently established in Greek community or insurance data in this bounded pass. There is no evidence for a low-competition product import thesis. At EUR69.90 retail, EUR15 would require a **calculated 21.46% of gross retail value**; this is only a feasibility illustration, not an observed affiliate rate. A multi-item kit cannot be treated as one qualifying product unless actual programme terms and line-item commissions support it.

**Search terms:** `αυτόματη διακοπή νερού διαρροή εξοχικό`, `έξυπνη βάνα νερού χωρίς υδραυλικό`, `αισθητήρας διαρροής ηλεκτροβάνα`, `Aqara VC-X01D`, `retrofit water shutoff valve`, `water leak shutoff holiday home`.

**Next test:** identify qualifying complete solutions in the stream, verify payout at product level, research Greek owner questions, and compare hub cost, valve fit and local support. Manufacturer limits must govern claims; do not convert water-control marketing into gas-safety advice. Falsify the commercial thesis if no actual product commission reaches EUR15 or local equivalent kits already satisfy the use case competitively.

## Evidence handoff to the streaming selector

1. Keep catalogue intake broad. Resolve an actual programme/product commission amount in EUR using its real commission basis. Unknown payout cannot silently pass EUR15, and it cannot be represented as zero demand.
2. For commission-qualified items, derive multilingual problem/feature queries from each item's actual attributes; these examples are seed queries, not a fixed category gate.
3. Persist support and contradiction separately. Record source URL, source/crawl date when shown, retrieval time, exact model/alias, geography, and evidence family. A third-party AliExpress archive is an external lead, not a verified AliExpress offer, ship-to-GR quote or affiliate contract.
4. Separate Greek **product availability**, Greek **functional substitutes**, and **content competition**. They are different measurements. A rare exact SKU can still face abundant substitutes and strong comparison-site competition.
5. Require additional direct Greek demand evidence and a repeatable competition audit before calling any hypothesis high demand/low competition. Then test actual qualified clicks, merchant conversion and approved commission before claiming profitability.

No numeric demand score, search volume, order count, commission rate, winner status or production result was invented. No full Greek or international marketplace scan is claimed. This bounded pass ends with falsifiable hypotheses and stronger negative evidence than positive scarcity evidence; that is a valid research outcome.
